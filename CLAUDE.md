# Working on this repo

**Before any UI, UX, or component work: read `DESIGN.md` in full.** It is the single source of truth for the product vision, tech stack, design system, and quality bar. Every screen, component, and interaction must be evaluated against it — especially §3 (Design Philosophy), §27 (UX Rules), §28 (Do NOT build a generic admin dashboard), and §35 (Pre-ship checklist).

If a decision is ambiguous, favor **clarity over decoration**, **simplicity over configurability**, and **consistency over novelty**.
